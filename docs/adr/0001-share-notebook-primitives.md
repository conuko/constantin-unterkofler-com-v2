# Share Engineering Notebook primitives while preserving semantic presentations

Work, Read, and Play will compose a shared family of Engineering Notebook visual primitives instead of using either unrelated interfaces or one configurable generic card. Their presentation modules remain separate owners of their distinct content, behavior, and semantic choreography; the shared layer owns only recurring visual anatomy and interaction affordances. Shared primitives own consistent reveal, focus, hover, and press behavior through common timing and easing tokens, while each presentation module composes its own meaningful entrance order. Portfolio Page identity begins on route entry after only a brief paint-safe lead-in, remains perceptible, and finishes before a collection reveals any entries; a collection may register its viewport state sooner, but waits at the shared page-identity boundary and starts without another idle delay. This deliberately reopens the earlier choices not to share a Work/Music card or a global motion catalog while avoiding abstractions that would erase meaningful differences between portfolio areas.
