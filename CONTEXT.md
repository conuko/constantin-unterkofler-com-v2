# Personal Portfolio

The personal portfolio presents Constantin Unterkofler's professional identity, work, background, musical interests, and contact routes.

## Language

**Site Header**:
The persistent portfolio region that presents Constantin's identity, primary wayfinding, and appearance control across viewport sizes.
_Avoid_: Navigation bar, top bar

**Portfolio Content**:
The curated public material that presents Constantin's identity, background, work, musical interests, and contact routes as coherent portfolio pages.
_Avoid_: Site copy, content catalog

**CV**:
The ordered account of Constantin's professional roles and education presented within the portfolio.
_Avoid_: Resume, career list

**Portfolio Page**:
A public destination that presents one coherent area of Portfolio Content under its own title and metadata.
_Avoid_: Page shell, route page
